Coach every prompt.
Beside the turn, not inside it.

coachline is a Claude Code plugin. A panel beside your conversation shows what each prompt leaves out and hands you a sharper one, which you can put straight into the prompt box. It runs on your own Claude subscription, with no API key and nothing hidden in Claude's turn.

> /plugin marketplace add teransarathchandra/coachline
> /plugin install coachline@coachline
Paste both into Claude Code. The panel opens at once.Per-platform setup

H

A hint while you type

Pause, and the line under Claude Code's prompt box shows how concrete your draft is, and the one thing it lacks most.

  • Worked out on your machine. The draft is never sent anywhere.
  • New to Claude Code? Hints also say why, until you have 50 prompts behind you.
  • Off with /coach hints off.

Hold a vague prompt at Enteropt-in

Turn on /coach hold on and a clearly vague prompt stops at Enter. The reason shows, and your text stays in the box.

  • Enter again within 30 seconds sends it as is.
  • Enter is never slowed: it only holds what the hint has already judged.
  • Off by default.
E

Claude's read of your prompt, beside the turn

While Claude works on your prompt, coachline asks Claude separately, on your subscription, what the prompt leaves out.

  • Concrete tips, and the skills and plugins you already have that fit.
  • An enhanced prompt, with [ASK: …] where only you know the answer.
  • For something new: tools, docs and reference sites from the web, each link checked first.
U

Use puts it in your prompt box

One press and the enhanced prompt replaces your draft, its gaps in bold. You fill them in and send it yourself.

  • Nothing is ever sent for you.
  • A draft already in the box is replaced only on a second press.
  • In the Claude Code pane, the desktop app and your editor.
C

Copy it, anywhere

The standalone panel runs beside any terminal: Windows Terminal, or /coach watch. There, c copies the enhanced prompt to paste into Claude Code.

  • The arrow keys move through every prompt of the conversation.
  • i shows habits Claude spotted in your history, and s turns a repeated request into a skill.
L

Keep the prompts that worked

When a prompt's turn finishes and your next two prompts don't correct it, it's kept, redacted, on your machine.

  • Type something like it later and the hint says similar prompt worked before.
  • The Library puts it back in the box, or copies it.
  • coachline also notes when you stop a turn or say "no, I meant…", and shows how often vague prompts led to rework.

What happens to your prompts

Exactly this, and nothing else.

No API key, no telemetry
Claude runs through your own Claude Code login. coachline has no server of its own.
Redacted before it is sent
Emails, keys and tokens are removed first, and pasted text is never sent. List a project in llm-off.txt to keep it out entirely.
Checked on your machine
Counts are recomputed locally, skill names must exist, and every link must load before you see it.
Deleted in one command
/coach forget all deletes what it keeps. /coach panel-ai off leaves only the local hints.
